# Lightweight templates

Adapt these templates freely. They are not mandatory formats.

## After-meeting work note

```markdown
# What the meeting means

## Conclusion or decision

## What the manager expects

## What I need to deliver

- ...

## Important unknowns

## Next actions

1. ...

## What I should confirm with the manager
```

## Current-work review

```markdown
# Current work review

## Verdict

READY / READY WITH GAPS / NOT READY

## What is already usable

## What is missing or off track

## Highest-value next actions

1. ...

## Evidence or decision needed from others
```

## Bad-case/good-case note

```markdown
| Bad case observed | Why it fails | Good case next time |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... |
```

## Meeting report

```markdown
# Meeting report

## Conclusion

## Three decisive findings

1. ...

## Important evidence and numbers

## Remaining gaps and their impact

## Recommendation and requested decision

## Likely questions

| Question | Short answer | Evidence or next verification |
|---|---|---|
```

## Multi-meeting synthesis

```markdown
# What changed across the meetings

## Stable manager expectations

## Repeated delivery gaps

## Decisions that changed

## Current real assignment

## Work for the next few days

1. ...

## How to report it
```

## Latest-meeting retrospective

```markdown
# What the latest meeting says about my previous work

## What was accepted and useful

## What was rejected, missing, or unclear

## Root cause

Missing work / weak evidence / wrong scope / poor explanation / unclear expectation

## What the manager now expects

## What I will do differently

## How the next meeting will avoid repeating the same loop
```

## High-bandwidth meeting plan

```markdown
# Next meeting

## Decision or outcome needed

## One-minute conclusion

## Evidence to pre-read

## Questions worth synchronous discussion

1. ...

## Topics to keep out of the meeting

## Requested decision or help

## Close

Decision / owner / next output / timing
```
