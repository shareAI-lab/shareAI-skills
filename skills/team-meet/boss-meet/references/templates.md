# Lightweight manager templates

Adapt freely. Do not require exact formatting.

## Meeting brief

```markdown
# Meeting brief

## Outcome needed

## Artifact or pre-read

## Questions worth synchronous time

1. ...

## Known blocker

## Desired close

Decision / correction / owner / next output
```

## Work-review agenda

```markdown
# Work review

1. Purpose and decision needed
2. Employee conclusion without detailed interruption
3. Evidence and assumptions
4. Real gap or disagreement
5. Decision or correction
6. Owner, output, timing, acceptance condition
```

## Direct feedback note

```markdown
# Feedback

## Observable result

## Expected result

## Impact

## Required change

## Next check
```

## Recent-meeting review

```markdown
# Meeting review

## Verdict

## What advanced the work

## What consumed time without enough value

## Employee-side issue

## Manager-side issue

## What should be async next time

## What deserves meeting time

## One change for the next meeting
```
